## 2. Upstream Data License

All public safety-and-health datasets retrieved through this MCP server
originate from the **Korea Occupational Safety and Health Agency (KOSHA,
한국산업안전보건공단)** via the Korean open-data portal **data.go.kr**.

These datasets are made available under the **Korea Open Government License
(KOGL, 공공누리)**. Each individual dataset may be tagged with a specific
KOGL type. The types relevant to this project are:

| Type | Label | Requirements |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 제1유형 (Type 1) | Attribution | Credit the source. Commercial use and modification allowed. |
| 제2유형 (Type 2) | Attribution + Non-commercial | Credit the source. Commercial use prohibited. |
| 제3유형 (Type 3) | Attribution + No-derivative | Credit the source. Modification prohibited. |
| 제4유형 (Type 4) | Attribution + Non-commercial + No-derivative | Credit the source. Both commercial use and modification prohibited. |

> **Always check the KOGL mark** on the original dataset page at
> `data.go.kr` (or the KOSHA portal) before redistributing or commercially
> reusing a specific piece of data.

The bundled seed datasets in `src/ontology/*.json` include a
`license` field that reflects the upstream type.

## 3. Required Attribution When Redistributing Data

When you expose data retrieved through this MCP to end users, you must
preserve the original attribution. A recommended citation is:

```
출처: 한국산업안전보건공단 (KOSHA)
공공데이터포털 — https://www.data.go.kr/
```

Each tool response from this MCP includes a `source` field and a
`_meta` block with `providedBy` and `developedBy` — those values are
safe to surface to users and satisfy the KOGL attribution requirement
in most cases.

For materials under **Type 3** or **Type 4** (no-derivative), you must
reproduce the original without modification. Summaries or paraphrases
generated by an LLM should not be presented as if they *are* the
upstream document.
